# Stride-Wise-AI

StrideAI is a free personal trainer app that tracks your workouts and gives you AI coaching built around your goals and injuries.









StrideAI is a personal trainer for your desktop. Log your workouts, watch your progress build, and get training guidance from an AI coach that adapts to your goals and recent activity.



Your dashboard shows your week at a glance: workouts completed, active time, distance covered and calories burned. Every session you log is added to your recent activity, so you can see how your training is going without any spreadsheets.



When you're not sure what to do next, ask the AI Coach. It builds a personalized, injury-aware plan around your goals, so you can train hard without aggravating old problems. You can also join the Community section to connect with other athletes and stay motivated.



StrideAI is useful for runners, gym-goers, and anyone who wants a simple, structured way to stay consistent.



Key features



Clean dashboard with weekly workouts, active time, distance and calories

Quick workout logging for running, training sessions and more

AI Coach that creates personalized, injury-aware training plans

Recent activity history to track your progress

Community section to connect with other users

Personal profile and settings

Runs in its own window with a system tray icon

Automatic updates through the installer



System requirements



Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit)

Internet connection required

About 250 MB of free disk space
